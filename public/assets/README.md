# Asset Folder Guide

Place your school images in the corresponding folders:

- `hero/` — Hero carousel images (slide-1.jpg, slide-2.jpg, etc.)
- `about/` — Campus photo and related images
- `staff/` — Staff profile photos (principal.jpg, deputy.jpg, etc.)
- `achievements/` — Top achiever photos by year
- `logo.png` — School logo/badge
